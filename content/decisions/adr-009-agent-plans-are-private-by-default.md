## Status

Accepted

## Context

Plans change rapidly and exposing every intermediate detail creates coordination noise.

## Decision

Keep detailed plans private to the Run. Share minimal Intent for coordination.

## Consequences

Agents retain flexibility while peers still get conflict-relevant signals.
